
# Space Exploration Quiz

Welcome to our Space Exploration Quiz project! This interactive project was developed as part of an inter-college science project competition, with guidance and support from my computer science teacher. The project showcases an engaging way to learn about space through an animated astronaut and a space-related quiz, utilizing HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Project Overview

The Space Exploration Quiz project aims to provide an educational yet fun experience for users interested in space. The project features an animated astronaut who guides users to a space-themed quiz, testing their knowledge and expanding their understanding of space science.

### Features

- **Animated Astronaut:** A visually appealing astronaut animation that captivates users and sets the theme.
- **Interactive Quiz:** A space-related quiz with multiple questions, providing immediate feedback to users.
- **Start Quiz Button:** A clear and inviting button to begin the quiz, enhancing user engagement.
- **Responsive Design:** Ensuring a seamless experience across various devices and screen sizes.

### Technologies Used

- **HTML:** For structuring the content and quiz elements.
- **CSS:** For styling the animation, quiz interface, and overall design.
- **JavaScript:** For implementing the quiz logic, animations, and interactivity.

## How to Use

1. Open the project in your web browser.
2. Watch the animated astronaut introduce the quiz.
3. Click the "Start Quiz" button to begin the space-themed quiz.
4. Answer the questions and receive immediate feedback on your knowledge of space.
5. Enjoy the interactive and educational experience.

Feel free to explore the code, try the quiz, and provide any feedback. Thank you for your interest in our Space Exploration Quiz project!
